Common Questions

A few things people usually ask.

Can I hire S&B for just one service, not all three?

Yes. Most clients start with one service — often cleaning or supplies — and add property care or the others later as it makes sense. Nothing is bundled by default.

Do you provide the cleaning products and supplies yourselves?

Yes, for cleaning service the products and equipment are included. For facility supply programs, we track usage and handle restocking on the schedule we set together.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing depends on property size, condition, service frequency, and which of the three services you need. We provide a walkthrough and written quote before anything is scheduled.
